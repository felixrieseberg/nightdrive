## New

- First public release of Nightdrive: play a folder of music and sync it with a
  classic click-wheel iPod, both ways.
- Browse by artist, album, genre, and playlist, edit song info, find duplicates,
  and organize the files on disk.
- Watch the deck: a fold-down display with swappable visualizers.
- Nightdrive now updates itself. Turn automatic checks off any time in
  Settings ▸ General ▸ Updates.
